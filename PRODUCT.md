# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Split audience — two distinct entry points, one site:

- **Recruiters / employers**: scanning for ICT Support Engineer credentials, Microsoft 365 depth, SharePoint and Power Automate experience. Time-pressured. Need to verify competence fast and feel like they're looking at someone serious.
- **Freelance clients**: business owners and startups evaluating a web developer for hire. Outcome-focused. Need to see real delivered work and understand what they'd be getting.

Both audiences land cold and decide in under 30 seconds whether to dig in or bounce.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio for Tanveer Singh — ICT Support Engineer and freelance full-stack developer based in Greater Sydney. Also a content creator (Twitch Affiliate, YouTube).

The site exists to convert cold visitors into qualified leads — either an interview request or a project inquiry. Success = the person reading it believes Tanveer can do the job before they close the tab.

## Brand Personality

Technical. Immersive. Electric.

The aesthetic is the credential. A recruiter landing on this site should feel they've entered an engineer's workspace — operational, deliberate, high-functioning — not a graphic designer's portfolio or a generic template. The Cyberpunk theme is the brand; it signals a builder who controls their environment down to the pixel.

Not "flashy" — specific. Every visual element should feel like it belongs to a system, not a mood board.

## Anti-references

- **Generic template portfolios**: white cards, hero images, skills percentage bars, Bootstrap-shaped grids. The "I followed a tutorial" feel.
- **Flashy gamer aesthetics**: RGB overflow, neon everything, looks like a gaming PC advertisement. Loud without substance.
- **Typical SaaS landing pages**: hero metric template, gradient text, identical icon+heading+body card grids, eyebrow labels on every section.
- **Corporate IT resume sites**: stiff, overly formal, zero personality. Indistinguishable from a PDF.

## Design Principles

1. **The environment is the first credential.** Before a visitor reads a word, the site's texture — boot sequence, console, scanlines, neon precision — signals that Tanveer controls his stack. The aesthetic does work.
2. **Precision over decoration.** Every visual effect earns its place by adding meaning or atmosphere. Nothing is there purely because it looks cool; it either communicates capability or reinforces the world.
3. **Dual-audience legibility.** Employers need to verify credentials in under 30 seconds. Clients need to see delivered outcomes. Structure and hierarchy serve both without diluting either path.
4. **Content feels like system output.** Copy, data, and lists should feel pulled from a live operational system — terminal-flavored, structured, scannable. Not typed into a form template.
5. **Motion signals activity, not entertainment.** Animations communicate system state — booting, loading, responding — not decoration. Boot sequences, state transitions, console feedback. Purposeful or absent.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum (4.5:1 body text contrast, 3:1 large text)
- Reduced motion support required for all animations (`prefers-reduced-motion`)
- Keyboard navigable — skip link already in layout
- Dark theme only (cyberpunk palette); no light mode planned currently
